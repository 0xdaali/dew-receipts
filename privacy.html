Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 (personal mailbox added)

dew-receipts is an internal tool operated by D.E.W. Capital Oy for its own bookkeeping. It is used only with the Google accounts of the company and of its owner.

What it accesses

With the Gmail read-only permission (gmail.readonly), the tool searches those mailboxes for purchase receipts and downloads those emails and their attachments. It cannot send, delete or modify email.

How the data is used

Receipts are stored on the company's own server and used only to record purchases and match them to the company's card transactions. Receipt contents may be processed by an AI model provider (Anthropic) to extract the merchant, date, amount and line items.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties beyond the processing described above. Google user data is not used for advertising or for training AI models.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from each Google account's security settings (Third-party apps & services).
